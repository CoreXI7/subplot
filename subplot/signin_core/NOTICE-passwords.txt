common-passwords.txt.gz: the common passwords Subplot's sign-in refuses.

Source: SecLists (https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists), commit cb6d7645abf4a2c467bc80f84cf70bda3d60c2d6,
Passwords/Common-Credentials/Pwdb_top-1000000.txt and 100k-most-used-passwords-NCSC.txt, filtered to the entries of
15 to 64 printable characters and lower-cased (Fishbowl's file, less one entry that looks like a personal name: 5530
entries). SecLists is MIT licensed.

qrcodegen.py: Project Nayuki's QR Code generator (https://github.com/nayuki/QR-Code-generator), python/qrcodegen.py at
commit 777682a64202fdb837b50e351b25b7ddb27852c4, unmodified; MIT (the licence is in the file's header). It only computes
the QR code shown when turning on an authenticator app.
